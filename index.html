Right now, it takes a dozen apps

One to chat. One to follow people. One to publish. One to get paid. Each one holds your data on its own servers, each one can change the rules, and none of them are yours.

Messengers

Private in the message, but your account, your address book and the servers still belong to someone else.

Social platforms

Your audience lives on their land, and a feed you do not control decides who actually sees you.

Creator platforms

You rent a shopfront, follow their rules, and pay a cut of everything you make there.

Cloud apps

Your files, notes and history sit on a server far away, and stop working when the connection or the company does.

Aerodash puts them under one roof, and puts you in charge of the roof.

One app. Six promises.

Aerodash is built on a few ideas that do not bend, because every feature has to rest on them.

End-to-end encrypted

Messages and content are locked on your device and opened only by the people they are for. Not by us, not by a relay, not by anyone in between.

Local-first

Your data lives on your own devices first. The app is fast, works offline, and treats the network as a convenience, not a requirement.

User-owned

Your identity, your keys and your data are yours. Take them with you, and the app keeps working even if any one company disappears.

User-operated

Run it yourself, or pick someone you trust to run it for you. There is no central service you have to depend on, and nobody who can switch you off.

Relay-driven

Relays carry encrypted messages and files between people and devices, like couriers who cannot read the mail. Anyone can run one, and you can use as many as you like.

Privacy-first

No ads, no tracking, no profile quietly built on you. What you share is what you choose to share, and nothing else leaves your hands.

Built for three kinds of people

Everyone gets the same app. What it is for depends on who is using it.

Creators

Publish, share and sell directly to the people who follow you, from a home you own. Reach them through relays you choose, with updates, support and newsletters built in, and no platform taking a cut of the middle.

Consumers

Talk, follow, read and keep what you love in one private app. Your messages, subscriptions and history stay on your devices, and you decide who can reach you and who cannot.

Curators

Gather, recommend and vouch for the things people should see. Build lists and feeds that others can follow, with a real audience, and no algorithm deciding who finds you.

A different way to run an app

Most apps run on a server someone else owns, and you are a guest on it. Aerodash turns that around.

The usual way

  • Your data lives on their servers
  • Your account, audience and history can be changed, closed or sold along with the company
  • Every message passes through a service that can see it
  • One app to chat, another to publish, another to get paid, each with its own account

The Aerodash way

  • Your data lives on your devices, encrypted
  • You hold the keys, and you can take your identity with you
  • Relays move messages along, and they cannot read them
  • One app, with mini-apps like Gondola for the rest, all under the same promises
